The PHI boundary, by design

Referring practice sees

  • Its own referred patients only
  • Status, documents owed, its account balance
  • No other practice's data, ever
  • No clinical notes, no internal scoring
  • No PHI in emails or links, only in session

Practice staff sees

  • Full patient record under a clinical role
  • The whole partner pipeline and scores
  • Dormant list and reactivation sequences
  • Every access written to the audit log
  • Governed by role profiles, not UI tricks

Design decisions, and what I would verify first

This preview is built to be defensible, not just to look finished. Below is how it maps to your Zoho and the questions I would answer against your live instance before building anything.

How this maps to your Zoho

Nothing new to re-platform

  • Portal is Accounts data, scoped per referring practice with role based sharing.
  • Case status reads the Deal stage, so the portal is never a second version of the truth.
  • A document request writes back as an Activity on the Account, so every touch is logged.
  • Balances come from the receivables feed, read only, scoped to that account.
  • Built with CRM Portals or Creator on your existing instance, in a sandbox first.
Compliance decisions

What a reviewer can approve

  • Minimum necessary: a practice sees its own referrals, status, documents, balance. No clinical notes.
  • Per account isolation enforced by sharing rules, confirmed by a deny test, not by hiding fields.
  • No PHI in notification subject lines, bodies, or URLs. Links resolve only after auth.
  • Audit trail on every view and request, retained per your policy.
  • Under your BAA, with role profiles for portal, front desk, and clinical.
What I verify first

Before touching the live instance

  • Which single field currently owns "who referred this," and where it doubles as service.
  • How the four pipelines in the Deals module actually differ, before any split.
  • The exact behavior change in the mid year boolean, with record samples.
  • The shape of the 4,700 row external ID file against current keys.
  • Every change in a sandbox with a rollback and a change log, nothing live blind.
Why it is built this way

One field, one job

  • The portal only works once each fact has one owning field, so the status it shows is trustworthy.
  • Reconcile source, value, and follow through to one record first, then expose a slice of it.
  • A portal on messy data just publishes the mess faster, so hygiene comes before access.
  • Documentation is written as the build happens, so anyone can maintain it after me.
